Support

PathLog is an honest, on-device trip logger: start a trip, lock your phone and go. It records route, time, distance, speed and battery — and tells you exactly what it knows. No account, no cloud, no analytics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I record a trip?

Tap Start Recording on the home screen and allow location access ("While Using"). Lock your phone and go — recording continues with the screen off. Tap End Trip when you arrive, then confirm with End & Save.

Why does a portion of my route show as a dashed line?

That is an honest gap: GPS signal was lost or too imprecise for that stretch. PathLog never invents a route — distances and speeds only count where real location samples exist.

What are the marked "stops"?

When several precise samples stay within a small range for a few minutes, PathLog marks a likely stop. Stops are estimates and are labeled as such — they are never presented as confirmed facts.

Does recording drain my battery?

Continuous GPS uses more power than normal use. PathLog only requests location while a trip is running and stops immediately when you end it. Battery level is shown alongside your route so you can see the real cost of each trip.

Where is my data stored? Can I lose it?

Entirely on your device — there is no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes all trips, so keep that in mind. Backups depend on your device's own backup settings; PathLog does not upload anything.

What is the home screen widget for?

The widget starts a recording and opens your trips. The lock screen Live Activity and Dynamic Island show live status — duration, distance and recent speed — while a trip is running.

支持与常见问题

PathLog 是一款诚实的本机行程记录器：开始行程、锁上手机就出发。它记录路线、时间、距离、速度和电量——并如实告诉你它真正记录到了什么。无账号、无云端、无统计分析。

常见问题

怎么记录一段行程？

在首页点「开始记录」并允许定位授权（"使用 App 期间"），然后锁上手机出发——锁屏状态下继续记录。到达后点「结束」保存。

为什么路线有一段是虚线？

那是诚实的空白：这段 GPS 信号丢失或精度太差。PathLog 绝不编造路线——距离和速度只按真实定位样本计算。

地图上标出的「停留」是什么？

当多个较精确的定位样本在小的范围内持续几分钟，PathLog 会标记一个疑似停留。停留是推测，界面会明确标注——不会被当成确定的事实。

记录会不会很耗电？

持续定位比日常使用更耗电。PathLog 只在行程进行中请求定位，行程结束立即停止。地图上会显示行程中记录到的电量，每次行程的真实开销一目了然。

数据存在哪里？会丢吗？

完全存在你的设备上——没有账号，也没有服务器。删除 App 会删除全部行程，请留意。备份取决于你设备自身的备份设置；PathLog 不上传任何东西。

桌面小组件是干什么的？

小组件可以开始记录并打开行程列表。行程进行中，锁屏实时活动和灵动岛会显示状态——用时、距离和最近区间速度。
