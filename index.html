Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Gathering Storm ("the App") is an offline note-taking app for iOS, provided by Underwater Volcano (Shanghai) Technology Co., Ltd ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles — in short: your notes never leave your device, and we do not collect any personal information.

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. It has no account system, no server, no analytics, and no advertising. It makes no network requests.

1. Information We Collect

None. We do not collect, store, or process any personal information on our side. There is no sign-up, no login, no tracking, and no third-party SDK of any kind in the App.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

All content you create in the App — messages, memos, spaces, and attached images — is stored only in the App's local storage on your device. We have no access to it, and it is never uploaded anywhere.

3. Permissions

The App may request two permissions, only when you use the corresponding features:

  • Camera — to take a photo and attach it to a note. The photo stays on your device.
  • Photo Library — to pick an existing photo and attach it to a note. The photo stays on your device.

Neither permission is required to use the App for text notes, and granting them never causes any data to leave your device.

4. Data Export and Deletion (Your Control)

  • Export: You can export all of your notes as a JSON file from Settings. The export is created on your device and is shared only if you choose to share it (for example, via AirDrop or saving to Files).
  • Delete: You can wipe all App data with one tap in Settings. Uninstalling the App also permanently removes all of its data from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no information at all, it does not knowingly collect any information from children. The App is safe for use by all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the App changes how data is handled, we will update this page and the App Store listing before that version is released.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: caizhidian@gmail.com

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 13 日

《暗流涌动》（Gathering Storm，以下简称"本应用"）是一款离线笔记应用，由上海水下火山科技有限公司（Underwater Volcano (Shanghai) Technology Co., Ltd）（以下简称"我们"）提供。本政策说明本应用处理信息的方式——简单来说：你的记录从不离开你的设备，我们不收集任何个人信息。

本应用不收集、不传输、不分享你的任何数据。 它没有账号系统、没有服务器、没有统计分析、没有广告，也不发起任何网络请求。

一、我们收集的信息

无。我们不在任何一侧收集、存储或处理你的个人信息。本应用没有注册、没有登录、没有追踪，也不包含任何第三方 SDK。

二、存储在你设备上的数据

你在本应用中创建的全部内容——消息、Memo、空间以及附件图片——仅保存在你设备上本应用的本地存储中。我们无法访问这些数据，它们也绝不会被上传到任何地方。

三、权限说明

仅当你使用对应功能时，本应用才会请求以下两项权限：

  • 相机——用于拍摄照片并附加到一条记录中。照片只留在你的设备上。
  • 照片图库——用于选择已有照片并附加到一条记录中。照片只留在你的设备上。

这两项权限都不是使用本应用记录文字所必需的；即使授予，也不会导致任何数据离开你的设备。

四、数据导出与删除（由你掌控）

  • 导出：你可以在设置中将全部记录导出为 JSON 文件。导出在你的设备上生成，只有当你主动选择分享时（例如通过隔空投送或存储到"文件"）才会离开设备。
  • 删除：你可以在设置中一键清空本应用全部数据。卸载本应用也会从你的设备上永久移除其全部数据。

五、儿童隐私

由于本应用完全不收集信息，我们不会有意收集任何儿童的任何信息。本应用适合所有年龄段使用。

六、政策变更

如果未来版本的本应用改变了数据处理方式，我们会在该版本发布前更新本页面及 App Store 页面。

七、联系我们

如果你对本政策有任何疑问，请联系：caizhidian@gmail.com
