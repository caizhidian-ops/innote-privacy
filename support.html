Support

Gathering Storm is a fully offline note-taking app: capture notes like sending messages, merge them into Memos, and file them into Spaces. All data stays on your device — no account, no server, no network.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device, in the App's local storage. Nothing is ever uploaded; the App makes no network requests.

How do I back up my notes?

Go to Settings → Export to export all of your notes as a JSON file. You can then save it to Files, iCloud Drive, or share it however you like.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Settings → Wipe data to erase all notes at once. Uninstalling the App also permanently removes all of its data.

How do I switch the language?

Go to Settings → Language and choose between 简体中文 (Chinese) and English.

How do I organize my notes?

Long-press to select several notes on the timeline, then tap New Memo to merge them into one Memo — the originals stay attached and expandable. File Memos into Spaces to keep projects, life and ideas organized. Use Search to find any old note by keyword.

Does the App have cloud sync, AI, or voice input?

Not in the current version. Version 1 focuses on fast, private, fully local note-taking. Because there is no account and no server, there is also nothing to sync or sign in to.

支持与常见问题

《暗流涌动》是一款完全离线的笔记应用：像发消息一样随手记，划选合并成 Memo，归档进空间。所有数据只存在你的设备上——无账号、无服务器、不联网。

常见问题

我的数据存在哪里？

完全存在你的设备上，位于本应用的本地存储中。任何数据都不会被上传，本应用也不发起任何网络请求。

如何备份我的记录？

进入「设置 → 导出」，可将全部记录导出为 JSON 文件，然后保存到"文件"、iCloud 云盘或通过任意方式分享。

如何删除全部数据？

进入「设置 → 清空数据」，可一键抹掉全部记录。卸载本应用也会从设备上永久移除其全部数据。

如何切换语言？

进入「设置 → 语言」，可在简体中文与 English 之间切换。

怎么整理我的记录？

在时间线上长按选中多条记录，点「新Memo」即可合并成一份 Memo——原始记录完整保留、随时展开。把 Memo 归入不同的空间，项目、生活、灵感各归各位。用「查找」可以按关键词搜到任何旧记录。

有云同步、AI 或语音输入吗？

当前版本没有。1.0 版专注做快速、私密、完全本地的记录。因为没有账号、没有服务器，自然也没有需要同步或登录的东西。
